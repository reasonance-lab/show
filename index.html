About Reasonance Lab

Reasonance Lab is an independent project portfolio created by Emil Jivishov. It brings together work in education technology, chemistry and scientific software, robotics, and applied AI. The projects are built to explore practical uses of modern browser technologies and AI while keeping the underlying workflows understandable and useful to their intended users.
